Vision

We envision a harmonius community for the favorable living conditions of the campus strays and UPV constituents, in consideration of both animal welfare and public health conditions.

Mission

Project PAWradise is committed to educating and raising awareness on animal welfare, specifically the Animal Welfare Act of 1998, otherwise known as RA 8485. We aim to meet the basic needs of the campus strays, including but not limited to food, fostering rehoming, vaccinations, and population control through ethical neutering.
